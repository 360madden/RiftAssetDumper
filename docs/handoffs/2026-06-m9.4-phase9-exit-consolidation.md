## Part 5: What Was NOT Done in Phase 9

### Deferred to Human Review

1. **Gate 5 reframing** (M7.4): The decision to accept `attrSets=0` as architectural rather than anomalous requires human review. The formal decision record is prepared at `docs/handoffs/2026-06-m7.4-formal-decision-record.md`.

2. **Gate 6 safety brake**: Intentionally held. Gates 1b and 2 clearance means 5 of 7 gates are now cleared, but gate 6 must remain BLOCKED until all gates (including gate 5) are resolved.

3. **Promotion flag changes**: Both `FieldOrderPromoted` and `ParserExportPromotionAllowed` remain false. These should only change after gates 5 and 6 are resolved.

### Deferred to Data-Level Probing

4. **`10010400`/`3c010400` sub-family distinction**: Both are mapped as "packed vertex attributes" at the family level. The specific attribute type (signed/unsigned, color/normal/bone-weight) requires data-level probing. This is a refinement, not a gate blocker.

### Not Needed

5. **Ghidra analysis**: The stride hypothesis (M9.1) and semantic map (M9.2+M9.3) were both proven without Ghidra — evidence is purely structural/payload-derived. This validates the per-block-embedded descriptor architecture: the descriptor bytes describe the stream body layout, not a static table lookup.
