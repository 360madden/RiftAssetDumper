## What the chain is

The §8.4 decision chain is the load-bearing judgment for closing (or re-opening) the `Step49ClosureMode` field in `docs/live-memory-step49-status.json`. The chain runs a phased live-memory scan against the user's `rift_x64.exe` to test the raw-contiguous-static-float3 representation hypothesis for a target mesh. The chain has 3 phases:

1. **Phase 1**: scan for asset ID ASCII strings (`6fc01704d4a509d5`, `caa9a88e94ec8db0`)
2. **Phase 2**: scan for the @264 index-strip prefix co-resident with a Phase 1 hit (±4 MiB), produces anchor `A`
3. **Phase 3**: scan for 4 known float3 triplets (v0..v3) within ±4 MiB of `A`

The Phase 3 result maps to Mode A (FAIL no surrogate) / Mode B (FAIL surrogate present) / Mode C (PASS partial) / Mode D (PASS full). Modes A/B keep `Step49ClosureMode: closed-negative-current-live-state`; Modes C/D flip it to `open-positive-live-partial` or `open-positive-live-confirmed`.
