## Blockers (carry forward to FT-2+)

1. **No real DDS decoder installed** — `pillow-dds` or ImageMagick `magick` needed for
   non-synthetic decode. Without this, FT-1's pipeline is proven but not exercised on
   real archive data. **Owner**: next session. **Fix**: `pip install pillow-dds`.
2. **RiftFlythrough ruff failures** (12 errors + 2 unformatted files) — pre-existing in
   `build_texture_map.py` and `convert_dds.py`. Not caused by FT-1.4's smoke. **Owner**:
   RiftFlythrough repo. **Fix**: separate sidecar task.
3. **nif-texture-links.jsonl has 9 entries** — FT-1.4's threshold of 10 wasn't met because
   the smoke uses unlinked synthetic PNGs. **Owner**: this repo. **Fix**: either expand
   the nif-texture-links generation, or relax the threshold to ≥9 for smoke runs.
