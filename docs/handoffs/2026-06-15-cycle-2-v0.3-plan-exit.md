### Lookup rules for the cohort

- `cohort.json` stores `asset_id` with `.world` suffix (e.g., `07f37c99a80da009.world`) because the cohort builder walks `objs/worlds/*.world.json` and uses the file stem
- The 4 bare-hex non-id IDs in `MESH_SIZE_OVERRIDES` (`07f37c99a80da009` etc.) are the lookup keys for `flythrough-index.json` + `scene-graph-manifest.json` enrichment
- To re-derive the cohort after `flythrough-index.json` changes: `python scripts/build_cycle_2_cohort.py --out Assets/Exports/discovery-plan/cycle-2/stage1/cohort.json`
